Video Formats ​

The required video-format attribute tells the player how to project and render your video.

Syntax ​

TypePattern
Equirectangularequirect-<fieldOfView>-<stereoType>
Fisheyefisheye-<fieldOfView>-<stereoType>
Flatflat-<stereoType>

Projection ​

OptionDescription
equirectEquirectangular projection.
fisheyeFisheye projection.
flatRegular rectangular video, no projection.

Field of view ​

For equirect video the angular extent of projection is 180 or 360. For fisheye video its 180.

Stereo type ​

OptionDescription
monoMonoscopic view.
lrStereoscopic. Left and right eye views side by side.
tbStereoscopic. Left and right eye views are stacked vertically, one on top of the other.

Examples ​

html
<commedia-player video-format="equirect-180-mono">...</commedia-player>
<commedia-player video-format="equirect-360-tb">...</commedia-player>
<commedia-player video-format="fisheye-180-lr">...</commedia-player>
<commedia-player video-format="flat-mono">...</commedia-player>
