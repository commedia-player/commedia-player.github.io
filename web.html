Frequently asked questions

Have questions? We're here to help.

How do I add Commedia to my website?
Follow the step-by-step instructions in our developer documentation. It covers installation and setup for your platform.
Do I need to be a developer to set it up?
The docs walk you through the whole process. If you get stuck, the Professional plan includes email support and Custom plan includes direct support, and you can always ask in our Discord.
Can I try Commedia before paying?
Yes. The Starter plan is free, so you can install the player and test it on your platform before upgrading. You can also try every addon module with a 10-minute trial per session, so you can see how they work before you subscribe.
Do you host my videos?
No. Commedia is the video player, and your videos stay on your own hosting or CDN.
Can people without a VR headset watch?
Yes. Commedia offers both 2D and VR modes. In 2D mode, viewers can use drag-to-look to explore 180° or 360° video without a headset.
How does yearly billing work?
The Professional plan is 79€ per month, billed yearly. You pay for the full year up front, which comes to 948€. Prices exclude VAT.
Can I upgrade or downgrade later?
Yes. You can change plans whenever your needs change, whether that means moving up to Professional or Custom as you grow or scaling back.
Do addons renew with my plan?
Yes. Your addons renew together with your plan, so everything stays on the same billing cycle.
What is the call-to-action screen?
It's a screen shown at the end of your video, in 2D mode only. You can add your own text and link, so viewers can click through to the next step, such as visiting a different section of your website or making a purchase. Available only in Professional and Custom plans.
Can I request a feature that isn't available?
Yes. We offer custom development. Contact us at business@commedia.app and tell us what you need.
Do you collect data about my viewers?
No. Commedia doesn't track your viewers. The player only checks your license and counts the number of view sessions, which is how your view session usage is measured.
What is considered a view?
A view is counted when a viewer watches at least 3 consecutive seconds of a video. If the viewer refreshes the page and watches again, it counts as a new view.
What happens if I go over my view limit?
We'll contact you and work with you on custom pricing that fits your needs.
Where can I get help?
It depends on your plan. Professional plan includes email support at business@commedia.app, and Custom includes direct support. Our developer documentation is available to everyone, and you can also reach the community and the team on Discord.
